What is Stormworks?

Stormworks is a sandbox vehicle-building game where players create boats, helicopters, planes, trains, and other machines using modular components and realistic physics.

The game combines creativity with engineering mechanics such as electricity, fuel systems, buoyancy, and logic systems.

Freedom of Expression

One of the main reasons Stormworks is interesting is the freedom players have while designing vehicles. Players can build realistic rescue vehicles, military-inspired machines, industrial systems, or completely experimental designs.

The game rarely forces players into one correct solution, which encourages creativity and experimentation.

About the Developers

Stormworks was developed by the Geometa. The developers continued expanding the game through updates and additional content after release. However they are developing a new game, which may replace Stormworks.

Buoyancy and Hulls

Buoyancy is one of the most important mechanics in Stormworks. Vehicle weight, hull shape, and balance all affect how ships behave in water.

Poor weight distribution can make vehicles unstable or even cause them to sink during rough conditions.

Electricity and Power Generation

Most vehicles in Stormworks rely on electrical systems for starting engines, powering equipment, and controlling vehicle components.

Players often use batteries, generators, solar panels, or reactors depending on the vehicle design.

Logic and Microcontrollers

Stormworks includes logic systems and microcontrollers that allow players to automate complex vehicle functions.

These systems can control navigation, stabilization, automatic doors, fuel management, warning systems, and much more.
